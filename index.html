You have 40,000
reviews. Now what?

Nobody is going to sit and read 40,000 reviews — not you, not your boss, not anyone. A machine can read all of them in seconds, but it won't do anything useful until you tell it which job you want done. Here's the good news: every single algorithm on your exam exists to do just one of three jobs. Once you can see the three jobs, the scary names turn into tools you actually know when to reach for.

Job 1Classify
Sort into boxes you knowYou already have the boxes in mind — positive or negative, spam or real — and some examples. You want new text sorted into those same boxes.
Job 2Cluster
Find groups nobody definedNo boxes yet. You hand the machine a pile of text and ask which pieces naturally belong together. It finds the groups; you name them after.
Job 3Discover
Surface the hidden themesYou don't want to sort or group — you just want to know what the text is about, pulling out the themes buried in thousands of documents.
